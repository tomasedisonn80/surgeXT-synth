## Result

The research supports the bank's organization around recurring musical roles.
The main document correctly separates those roles from an industry popularity
ranking, actual composer endorsement, acoustic realism, and approval of the
new presets. **No material factual correction to its composer-practice claims
was identified in this review.** No preset recipe or sound-state change is
recommended on the basis of this source check.

This was an independent retrieval of the cited interviews, cue breakdowns,
official collaborator products, and technical documentation. It was not a
listening test, a survey of score sessions, or a new native audio measurement.
Access date is distinct from publication date: several useful sources describe
older scores, and most vendor pages do not display a reliable publication date.
The collection therefore should not be advertised as a measured ranking of
2026 industry usage.

## Musical implications and acceptance wording

The ten categories are a defensible coverage and navigation scheme. An equal
100 patches per category is an authoring decision, not a measured distribution
of demand. The documented 200 families with five variants each should remain
explicit; variants can provide useful articulations without pretending to be
unrelated synthesis architectures.

The strongest common design lesson is that a palette must serve a scene.
Useful adaptation includes changing register, voicing, articulation, intensity,
motion, and ambience. A bright bass can become restrained; a short motif can
be made more sustained. Those controls do not convert every patch into a
convincing acoustic soloist or ensure that every cue benefits from that sound.
Hart's limited selection and Quayle's contrasting project palettes make this
boundary especially clear.

Continue to use **researched scoring roles**, **original presets**, and
**measured technical behavior** as supported claims. Retain human musical
audition as a separate pending status until actual listening records exist.
Neither source interviews nor passing native tests justify “Hollywood-approved,”
“definitely used by Hollywood composers,” “most-used industry presets,” or
“suitable for every genre without qualification.” No new market-wide adoption
data, composer endorsement, or subjective approval was obtained in this audit.

No modifications were made to `RESEARCH.md`, generated presets, recipes, audio
evidence, or packaging by this assignment.
